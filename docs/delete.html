Delete your HydroDrop Duo data

Last updated November 4, 2026

In Settings, then Duo Streaks, choose Delete my Duo data and confirm. When the request succeeds, it ends every duo you are in and deletes your name, droplet, days, nudges, notification token and phone key from our service, for this phone and every other device that shares your Duo Streaks. If your phone is offline, deletion must wait until it can reach the service.

Your partners are told the duo ended because you deleted your Duo data, without your name.

This works even if we have barred you from Duo Streaks.

What may remain temporarily

A few traces stay after that, each for a limited time.

A setup reservation can also remain after you delete your Duo data. After a completed setup it holds only the random phone ID, used status and its creation and expiry times. After a cancelled setup it also holds a keyed fingerprint of the setup secret, so a repeated cancellation can be checked. It cannot recreate the deleted identity. An hourly cleanup removes it after its original 24-hour expiry; deletion or cancellation does not restart that clock.

The notifications we already sent your partners stay in our notification log for up to three days after each was sent, with your first name as each one carried it, so we can look into a report.

Our logs keep a short scrambled tag of your Duo identity, never your name, for seven days.

If a request from your phone was already on its way when you tapped Delete, it can leave a counter we use to limit how often a phone does things, such as how many invites or nudges it made that day. Each holds only random ids, scrambled codes and a count, and goes when its hour or day is over, at most about three days later.

If a partner made a reconnect code for you, our record of their request keeps the random id our service gave your Duo identity, never your name, until an hourly cleanup deletes it 24 hours after the code was made.

If we looked into a report about a duo you were in, we may have made a separate copy of that duo's records in our storage on Cloudflare: the duo itself (when it started and how it ended), each person's first name, droplet, Support ID, days and nudges, when each of you started Duo Streaks, accepted its rules and last used HydroDrop, whether we have barred either of you, the invites made for it (never their codes), our notification log for that duo, and the random ids our service gave your Duo identities and phones. It is deleted at most about two days after we made it.

Our database's rolling backup keeps a copy for up to 30 days.

A keyed fingerprint of a pair that left or blocked can stay for up to a year to enforce the pairing restriction. It contains no names or raw Duo identity IDs.

If you no longer have the app

If you reinstall HydroDrop and it recovers your existing Duo identity, use Delete my Duo data. If it does not recover that identity, contact support with your previously saved Support ID. Starting a new Duo identity does not delete the old one. Without further activity, the old duos end after 30 days and their data is removed on the schedule above, within about 61 days after the last duo ends, apart from the backup copy and pair fingerprint described there.

If you need help deleting your Duo data, email support@hydrodrop.us with the Support ID from Settings, then Duo Streaks, then Diagnostics, if you still have the app; we act on these requests within 24 hours.
