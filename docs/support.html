HydroDrop Support

Need help with HydroDrop? The fastest way is directly from the app: open Settings → Report a Bug and send us the details. You can also email brownjonathon943@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No — HydroDrop works entirely on your device, no sign-up required.
Is my data backed up?
Your log is currently stored only on this device. iCloud sync across devices is a planned HydroDrop+ feature.
How do I cancel my HydroDrop+ subscription?
Open Settings → HydroDrop+ → Manage Subscription in the app, or manage it directly under your Apple ID → Subscriptions in the iOS Settings app.
