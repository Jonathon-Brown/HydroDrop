Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. HydroDrop works entirely on your device, with no sign-up required.
Is my data backed up?
Yes. Your log lives on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud account. That keeps it in sync between your iPhone and Apple Watch and restores it on a new device. HydroDrop has no servers of its own and never sees your data.
What does HydroDrop+ include?
HydroDrop+ removes ads, extends your History chart from the last 7 days to the last 30, and adds the Apple Watch app and the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. It protects your streak with one automatic freeze a month, makes reminders pace-aware so they stay quiet when you are already ahead, and adds a weekly recap, hot-day suggestions and a Live Activity. It also unlocks four more mascot skins, each with a matching Home Screen icon. Subscribing supports continued development of the app.
My widget shows a lock
Widgets are part of HydroDrop+. If you are subscribed and still see the lock, open HydroDrop once so it can refresh the widget. If that does not help, open the HydroDrop+ screen and tap Restore Purchases.
I subscribed but my features are missing
Open the HydroDrop+ screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.
How do I cancel my HydroDrop+ subscription?
Open Settings, then HydroDrop+, then Manage Subscription in the app. Or manage it under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions, in the iOS Settings app.
How do I remove the ads?
The free version shows a small banner ad. HydroDrop+ removes ads completely.
I am not getting reminders
Check that notifications are allowed for HydroDrop in iOS Settings, that Reminders is on in the app, and that the current time falls inside your reminder window.
