What HydroDrop is

A friendly water tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. A little droplet gets happier as you drink, reminders arrive only while you are awake, and your log stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Already have the app?

Then this page opened because the tag is new to your iPhone. Open HydroDrop, go to Settings, then My Bottles, and write the tag from there. After that, a tap logs the bottle straight away.
