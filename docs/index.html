A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your first sip.

Do I need an account?
No. HydroDrop works entirely on your device, with no sign-up required.
Does Say it work on my iPhone?
Say it requires iOS 26 and an available Apple Intelligence model. You can always use the quick-add buttons or enter an amount manually.
Can I use HydroDrop on Apple Watch?
Yes. The Apple Watch app is included with HydroDrop+.
What happens to my water log?
Your log stays on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud account.
