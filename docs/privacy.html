Privacy Policy for HydroDrop

Last updated July 22, 2026

HydroDrop does not collect, store, or share any personal data on any server. Everything you enter — your water intake, daily goal, and reminder settings — stays on your device using Apple's standard local storage and is never transmitted anywhere.

Bug Reports

If you use the "Report a Bug" feature in Settings, the app opens your device's Mail app with a pre-filled message containing basic diagnostic information (app version, iOS version, device model) and whatever you choose to write. This is sent directly from your device only if you tap Send — HydroDrop itself does not transmit this information anywhere.

In-App Purchases

HydroDrop+ subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment information. Apple's own privacy policy governs that data: apple.com/legal/privacy.
