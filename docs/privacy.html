Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version: HydroDrop has no sign-up. Your water log stays on your device and in your own private iCloud, and we never see it. If you choose Duo Streaks, your first name, your droplet and a little about each day go to our service so your partner can see them. We can see those shared details too.

On your device and in iCloud

Apart from Duo Streaks, described below, HydroDrop does not send anything you enter to a server of ours. Everything you enter, including your water intake, daily goal and reminder settings, is stored on your device using Apple's standard local storage.

If you are signed in to iCloud, your water log and preferences are also stored in your private iCloud account so they stay in sync across your devices. If you use Apple Health, it also records which Health entry each drink was saved as, the amount and time it was saved with, and which of your devices looks after it, so your devices can keep Health up to date without adding a drink twice. Nothing read from Apple Health is stored in iCloud. That data is protected by Apple's iCloud privacy terms and is never visible to us.

Apple Health

HydroDrop can add the drinks you log to Apple Health as dietary water. This is turned off until you turn it on in Settings, and iOS will ask your permission before HydroDrop can write anything.

Writing and reading are separate, and both are off until you turn them on. HydroDrop writes water, and caffeine if you turn on caffeine tracking. It reads nothing from Apple Health unless you connect Insights, and it can never see what other apps have written there beyond the four things listed below.

Insights is part of HydroDrop+. If you connect it, iOS asks your permission first, and HydroDrop then reads four things: your sleep, your resting heart rate, your workouts and your active energy. They are used on your device to show how they line up with the days you hit your water goal, and to suggest extra water on a day you worked out. None of those readings are saved by HydroDrop, synced to iCloud, shared with anyone, shown in a widget or sent to us, and they are never used for advertising. If you accept extra water after a workout, today's goal goes up, exactly as it does on a hot day, and that raised number is the only thing kept: on your device, and on your widget and watch for the rest of the day. Insights describes patterns in your own data. It is not medical advice. If you say no, everything else in HydroDrop works exactly as before.

What HydroDrop writes is the amount it counts towards your goal, so a coffee adds what it actually hydrates rather than the full cup. If you edit a drink, its Health entry is usually replaced by the device that added it, the next time HydroDrop opens there, even if you made the edit on another device. If that device hasn't done it within about a week, for example because its setting is off, another of your devices that has the setting on replaces the entry. If you delete a drink, the device you delete it on removes its Health entry if the setting is on there, and leaves a deletion record, described below, so that your other devices that have the setting on usually remove any entry they hold for it when HydroDrop next opens there, within 30 days. The setting is separate on each device: turning it off stops that device writing to Health and leaves whatever is already there alone. Another of your devices that has it on still adds the drinks you log on that device, once iCloud has synced them. You can remove it yourself at any time in the Health app, under Sharing, then Apps and Services, then HydroDrop.

To help your other devices remove a deleted drink's Health entries, HydroDrop keeps a small deletion record on your devices and, if iCloud is on, in your private iCloud. It contains references to the entries, whether they are water or caffeine, when deletion was requested, and which devices have handled it. Records become due for cleanup after 30 days and are removed when the app can next run Health syncing with the necessary access; they can remain longer if syncing is off or the app cannot run. Your Health store and private iCloud are protected by Apple's privacy terms. These records and your Health readings are never sent to us or the Duo service.

Location and weather

HydroDrop+ can suggest drinking a little more on hot days. This is turned off until you turn it on, and iOS will ask your permission before HydroDrop can use your location.

When it is on, HydroDrop asks the device for a rough location, accurate to about a kilometre, and sends it to Apple's weather service to ask what the conditions are. That is the only thing it is used for. Your location is not stored, is not kept in your water log, and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Apple's privacy terms cover the weather request.

A suggestion only ever offers extra water for that day, and only if you accept it. Your saved daily goal is never changed on your behalf. Turning the setting off stops HydroDrop asking for your location at all.

Duo Streaks

Starting a duo

Duo Streaks lets you keep a streak with one other person. Before you start, join or reconnect a duo, the app asks you to confirm your birth month and year, accept the Duo Streaks rules, and choose whether to continue. A new Duo identity is created only after you tap Create, Join or Reconnect. If you open an invite link or type a code after the age check, the code is sent to our service so the app can show you who invited you. If your phone already uses Duo Streaks, the app sends your phone's key with the code. Codes that don't work then count against your limit on wrong codes.

Each duo is one streak with one other person. With HydroDrop+ you can be in up to five, and each is kept separate on our service: the person in one duo cannot see your others.

What is shared

Before creating a Duo identity, our service reserves a random phone ID and keeps a keyed fingerprint of the secret your phone made, the device platform, app version, accepted rules version, and the reservation’s creation and expiry times. This lets the app recover from an interrupted setup without creating a second identity. The reservation can authorize setup for 24 hours. When setup succeeds, the reservation keeps only its random ID, used status and times. If setup is cancelled, it also keeps the keyed fingerprint so a repeated cancellation can be checked. These records cannot recreate a deleted identity and are removed by an hourly cleanup after the original 24-hour expiry.

When you use Duo Streaks, your phone sends these things to a service we run at api.hydrodrop.us: a random key that identifies your phone; your first name, if you typed one; your droplet; for each of the last few days, whether you met your saved daily goal and how far along you were, rounded down to a quarter; the nudges you send, which are chosen from eight fixed lines; the token Apple gives us so we can send your phone a notification, with your time zone and, while Duo notifications are on, your reminder hours; and which version of HydroDrop your phone runs and that it is an iPhone. Our service adds the time it received each day's entry. It also records that you accepted the Duo Streaks rules, which version, and when.

Your partner sees your first name, your droplet, each day's goal-met flag and quarter, when your phone last updated that day, and your nudges. We can see all of it too, because it is stored on our service in readable form. Our service also sees that your two phones are paired and when each one checks in.

It never receives what you drank, exactly how much, the size of your goal, anything from Apple Health, your location, your email address or your phone number. When you start or join a duo, your last week is shared the same way, so days you had both already met can count. The streak counts only what you log in HydroDrop. If you raise your goal, your last week is judged against the new goal, and days you no longer reach count as missed for your duo too.

Invites

An invite is an eight-character code. It is also carried in a link and a QR code. It works for 48 hours, once. Our service deletes an invite a day after it expires, whether it was used or not; an hourly cleanup does this within the hour.

Anyone who opens the link or types the code before it is used sees your first name and droplet and can join, so send it only to the person you mean. Someone you have blocked, or who left a duo with you, can't join with it while they use the same Duo identity. On a phone without it, they see your first name and droplet like anyone else, and if they join from there, we can't tell it's them. The first person to join is your partner; anyone after that is told the invite is no longer open and sees nothing.

If the wrong person joined, use Block on the Duo Streaks screen. It ends the duo. For a year, none of your invites will pair you with them again, even one you send them on purpose. That holds only while you both keep the same Duo identities: once either of you deletes your Duo data, or they start again on a phone without theirs, our service can't tell it's the two of you. Leave ends the duo too, but it can be undone: a new invite you make after leaving pairs whoever uses it, that person included. For the first day after someone joins, the duo also shows Not them? Leave & block, which does the same as Block.

Opening an invite link on a phone without HydroDrop loads a page on this website; the code stays in your browser and is not sent to the site or to us. The link also carries a second secret that your two phones keep for a future update; our service never receives it.

A new phone

If you lose your phone, a partner can make a reconnect code for you. They can make it only once none of your phones has checked in with our service for three days, and it works only if that is still true when it is used. Anyone who opens a reconnect code before it is used sees your partner's first name and droplet and your first name, so it should go only to you. Using it on your new phone, after you tap Reconnect, moves your Duo Streaks there, every duo you are in included. Every partner you share an active duo with is told, without your name, that your duo moved to a new phone.

Your old phone is disconnected. If Duo notifications are on there, it is sent one notice, with no name, that your duo moved, and nothing else. For 30 days it can still leave a duo, block, or delete your Duo data, so if the move wasn't you, you can still end your duos from it. Our service keeps your old phone's key until your Duo data is deleted, by you or on the schedule below. It deletes the old phone's notification token within a day after that phone has gone 90 days without checking in, or sooner if your last duo ends first.

Notifications

Duo notifications are sent by our service through Apple's notification system, so Apple carries them. A notification about a nudge carries your partner's first name and which of the eight lines they chose. One about a met goal carries their first name and whether you have met your own goal today, where you are, when it is sent. If several of these were held until your reminder hours start, they come as one summary for each duo, with your partner's first name. Notifications that a duo started, ended or moved to a new phone carry no name. None of them carries what either of you drank or how much.

We give Apple an expiry time for each notification: at most twelve hours after a nudge was sent, the end of that day everywhere on Earth for a met-goal alert, six hours for a summary, and up to a week for a notice that a duo started, ended or moved to a new phone. Delivery also depends on Apple and your device settings.

While Duo notifications are on, we use your reminder hours and time zone so we do not disturb you outside them. We also use your time zone to tell which day it is for you when your partner meets their goal. When you turn Duo notifications off, we stop keeping your reminder hours but keep your time zone. We use it to time the notice that a duo ended between 8 in the morning and 10 at night where you are. If you try the sample duo, its day also follows the time zone your phone last sent; it uses UTC if no time zone is available.

While Duo notifications are on, our service holds every Duo notification that would reach you outside your reminder hours until they start, using the hours and time zone your phone last sent it. Anything out of date by then is dropped instead of sent, such as a nudge more than twelve hours old, a nudge after you have met your goal, or a met-goal alert once that day is over everywhere on Earth. Turning off Duo notifications on the Duo Streaks screen tells our service to stop sending you every Duo notification but one: you will still be told when a duo ends, so the app can tidy up. If our service has no time zone for your phone, it sends that notice straight away. Once your last duo has ended, the app tells our service to forget your notification token. If it can't, our service deletes the token itself within about two days.

When a duo ends, HydroDrop removes its other notifications from your phone. The notice that it ended stays until you dismiss it. Delete my Duo data and Start fresh remove every Duo notification. Our service keeps a log of the notifications it sends you and your partners for three days, including the first name each one carried, so we can look into a report about it. An hourly cleanup then deletes it.

Leaving and retention

Our service deletes old data in cleanups that run every hour or every day. So each thing below goes up to an hour, or for the daily cleanups up to a day, after the time we give. Either of you can leave, and leaving ends the duo for both. If you two share more than one duo, leaving one ends them all. For a year after a leave, you can pair again only through a new invite from the person who left. That holds only while you both keep the same Duo identities: once either of you deletes your Duo data, or starts again on a phone without yours, our service can't tell it's the two of you.

When you leave, your days, your nudges and your name are removed from that duo at once; the other person keeps their own days and their view of the ended duo for 30 days, after which the whole duo is deleted. In each duo we keep only the days that are part of your current streak, plus the last nine days, up to 100 days; a daily cleanup deletes anything older. Nudges are deleted two days after they are sent, or at once if the person who sent them leaves or deletes their Duo data.

For Duo changes that support retries, our service keeps a short record so repeating the request does not repeat the change. It deletes that record after 24 hours. A record for a new invite holds a sealed copy of its code, which our service can open. A duo in which one of you has not used HydroDrop for 30 days ends by itself, as does a duo nobody joined within 14 days; anyone in it is told.

When your last duo is deleted, your name and droplet are removed in the same step. Your phone's key, and everything else our service keeps about your phones, is deleted 30 days after that. So your name, your key and everything else tied to you are gone within about 61 days after your last duo ends, or about 91 days after you last used Duo Streaks if your duos ended because you stopped using it. Two things can outlast that: our database’s rolling backup, for up to 30 days, and, if you or a partner left or blocked a duo, a keyed fingerprint of the pair for up to a year after the latest leave or block. The fingerprint contains no names or raw Duo identity IDs; our service uses it to enforce the pairing restriction.

Your notification token usually goes sooner: the app asks our service to forget it when your last duo ends, and if it can't, our service deletes it within about two days. A phone that has gone 90 days without checking in loses its token. If a notice that a duo ended is still waiting to be sent to that phone, our service keeps its token until the notice has gone, at most about three days after the duo ended. If your phone made a key but never got into a duo, for example because a join didn't go through, the key is deleted 30 days after it last checked in.

Deleting HydroDrop does not leave a duo, so leave it in the app first. If you forget, the duo ends by itself as described above and everything is removed on the same schedule. A block is tied to that person's Duo identity, not to a phone. If they delete their Duo data, or start again on a phone without that identity, our service sees someone new, and the block no longer covers them: any open invite of yours that reaches them can pair you again, and so can one of theirs that you join. A block works both ways: while it lasts, neither of you can join the other's invites.

Deleting your Duo data

In Settings, then Duo Streaks, choose Delete my Duo data and confirm. When the request succeeds, it ends every duo you are in and deletes your name, droplet, days, nudges, notification token and phone key from our service, for this phone and every other device that shares your Duo Streaks. If your phone is offline, deletion must wait until it can reach the service. Your partners are told the duo ended because you deleted your Duo data, without your name. This works even if we have barred you from Duo Streaks.

A setup reservation can also remain after you delete your Duo data. After a completed setup it holds only the random phone ID, used status and its creation and expiry times. After a cancelled setup it also holds a keyed fingerprint of the setup secret, so a repeated cancellation can be checked. It cannot recreate the deleted identity. An hourly cleanup removes it after its original 24-hour expiry; deletion or cancellation does not restart that clock.

A few traces stay after that, each for a limited time. The notifications we already sent your partners stay in our notification log for up to three days after each was sent, with your first name as each one carried it, so we can look into a report. Our logs keep a short scrambled tag of your Duo identity, never your name, for seven days. If a request from your phone was already on its way when you tapped Delete, it can leave a counter we use to limit how often a phone does things, such as how many invites or nudges it made that day. Each holds only random ids, scrambled codes and a count, and goes when its hour or day is over, at most about three days later. If a partner made a reconnect code for you, our record of their request keeps the random id our service gave your Duo identity, never your name, until an hourly cleanup deletes it 24 hours after the code was made. Deleting your Duo data does not clear the flag Apple keeps for us that your iPhone has made a Duo key, described under Who handles the service.

If we looked into a report about a duo you were in, we may have made a separate copy of that duo's records in our storage on Cloudflare: the duo itself (when it started and how it ended), each person's first name, droplet, Support ID, days and nudges, when each of you started Duo Streaks, accepted its rules and last used HydroDrop, whether we have barred either of you, the invites made for it (never their codes), our notification log for that duo, and the random ids our service gave your Duo identities and phones. It is deleted at most about two days after we made it.

Our database's rolling backup keeps a copy for up to 30 days. A keyed fingerprint of a pair that left or blocked can stay for up to a year to enforce the pairing restriction. It contains no names or raw Duo identity IDs. If you reinstall HydroDrop and it recovers your existing Duo identity, use Delete my Duo data. If it does not recover that identity, contact support with your previously saved Support ID. Starting a new Duo identity does not delete the old one. Without further activity, the old duos end after 30 days and their data is removed on the schedule above, within about 61 days after the last duo ends, apart from the backup copy and pair fingerprint described there.

If you need help deleting your Duo data, email support@hydrodrop.us with the Support ID from Settings, then Duo Streaks, then Diagnostics, if you still have the app; we aim to act on these requests within 24 hours. The same steps are at hydrodrop.us/delete.

Who handles the service

Our service runs on Cloudflare, Inc.'s network, which stores the data and, like any host, sees the connection details of each request, including your IP address, in order to deliver it and to stop floods. Its database is placed in North America, which Cloudflare treats as a preference rather than a guarantee.

Our own logs keep request ids, routes, error codes, how long each request took, and the app version and the device platform, for seven days. They also keep a short scrambled tag of your Duo identity, so we can follow its requests. They never hold your IP address, your name, a token, an invite code or the contents of a request. To limit how many Duo keys and checks one network can start in a day, our service keeps a scrambled form of your IP address, never the address itself, which changes every day. It is deleted within an hour after that day ends (UTC). To limit how often a phone can do things, our service counts, for each hour or day, its requests, the duos and invites it starts, the wrong codes it tries and the nudges it sends. Each count is kept under random ids, and for a wrong code under a scrambled form of the code, and goes within about three days.

Notifications pass through Apple's notification service under its terms, as described above. The iPhone app can ask Apple App Attest to vouch that it is a genuine copy of HydroDrop when it creates its key, starts a duo, joins one, moves to a new phone, or first sends us a new notification token. App Attest verification on our service is not switched on yet. The service does not read the attestation answer and keeps none of it.

Apple also keeps a flag for us about each iPhone that uses Duo Streaks (DeviceCheck): that it has made a Duo key. Our service asks Apple to set it when your iPhone makes a Duo key, if Apple answers at that moment. Deleting your Duo data does not clear it. Apple keeps it on its own servers, tied to that iPhone, so it also stays when HydroDrop is deleted or the iPhone is erased or sold. This version of HydroDrop never clears it, so it also applies to anyone who uses that iPhone later. If Apple tells our service the flag is already set when your iPhone makes a Duo key, our service allows only one such new key a day from the same network, and gives the key lower limits on requests, new duos, invites, wrong codes and nudges, such as one nudge a day in each duo instead of three. It keeps that setting with the key, and the lower limits cover your whole Duo identity while that key is one of its phones. That includes a key made on the same iPhone after you delete your Duo data. DeviceCheck also offers a second flag, for marking an iPhone we have barred; this version of HydroDrop never sets it.

The invite page and deletion page are served by the same website host as the rest of this site.

Age eligibility

Duo Streaks is not for anyone under 13. Before you start, join or reconnect a duo, HydroDrop asks for your birth month and year and shows them again for confirmation. It allows Duo only after the whole month of your thirteenth birthday has ended. Earlier year-only approvals must be checked again before Duo networking and partner widgets resume. Your birth month and year stay in memory while that screen is open. They are not saved or sent to us. The phone keeps only the eligibility decision and the version of the age check. A refusal is intended to remain on that phone; we cannot guarantee it survives every storage failure, reinstall or restore. If the age check refuses someone on a phone that holds a Duo identity, HydroDrop tries once to delete that identity's Duo data from our service, as Delete my Duo data does, but only if the identity was made by this installation of HydroDrop or was confirmed as theirs when the app asked whether to continue as them. Any other Duo identity on the phone, such as one that came back with a reinstall or a backup, is not deleted automatically, because it may be someone else's.

Copies on your phone and diagnostics

Each phone also keeps its own copy of the duo, including the other person's first name, droplet, days and nudges, which is what the Duo widget and duo notifications show. After a duo ends, that copy stays on the phone until whoever has it clears the ended duo on the Duo Streaks screen or deletes HydroDrop.

Diagnostics on the Duo Streaks screen shows your Support ID and recent sync information. Export diagnostics includes your Support ID when available, app and iOS versions, service state, queue count, notification settings, time zone, reminder hours, sync times, sequence numbers, short duo identifiers, error codes, HTTP statuses and filtered request IDs. It does not include your first name, drink log, invite codes, device secret or push token. The report opens in Mail and is sent only if you tap Send; your email address and anything you add to the message travel with it.

Advertising

The free version of HydroDrop shows banner ads served by Google's AdMob, which funds HydroDrop's development so the app doesn't require a subscription to use. HydroDrop+ removes the ads, whether you subscribe or buy HydroDrop+ Lifetime.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, HydroDrop shows no ads and does not start Google's ad software. It works this out on your device from your App Store country and your device's region setting, and sends neither anywhere to do so.

HydroDrop asks Google for non-personalized ads only. The ads you see are chosen from context, such as the app you are using and your rough region, and not from a profile of you or of what you do in other apps and websites. HydroDrop does not ask for permission to track you, so iOS does not give your device's advertising identifier to the app or to AdMob.

To deliver and measure ads and to stop fraud, AdMob still receives general technical information, such as your IP address, your device type and whether an ad was shown or tapped. Google's privacy policy governs this data.

Earlier versions of HydroDrop asked for permission to track in order to show more relevant ads. Current versions do not use that permission. If you answered that prompt, you can review or reset it at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Bug reports

If you use the Report a Bug feature in Settings, the app opens your device's Mail app with a pre-filled message containing basic diagnostic information (app version, iOS version, device model) and whatever you choose to write. This is sent directly from your device only if you tap Send. The message goes from your own email account. Duo diagnostics include additional fields listed in the Duo Streaks section.

In-app purchases

HydroDrop+ subscriptions and the one-time HydroDrop+ Lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs that data.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no sign-up. Its images, scripts and styles are served from this website, and its typefaces use the fonts already on your device. If you choose a light or dark theme, that preference is saved in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to us or used for tracking.
